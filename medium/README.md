## 1. To Respond to Customer Queries

> **Prompt:** Act as a customer support specialist. Respond to a customer query regarding [problem] they are facing with [product or service]. Your response should be empathetic, professional, and solution-oriented. Address the customer's concerns directly, provide clear instructions or information to resolve the issue, and reassure them about your commitment to their satisfaction. Ensure that the response complies with the company's policies and standards for customer service, and offer additional assistance or resources if necessary. Your goal is to resolve the customer's issue efficiently while maintaining a positive and supportive interaction.

## 2. To Handle Complaints

> **Prompt:** Act as a Customer Support Specialist. Your task is to handle a customer [complaint] with professionalism and empathy. The key is to actively listen to the customer's issue, acknowledge their frustration, and offer a solution that aligns with the company's policies while ensuring customer satisfaction. The response should be clear, courteous, and aimed at resolving the issue promptly. Document the complaint and the resolution steps taken, and follow up with the customer to confirm their satisfaction.

## 4. To Craft a Product Sales Page
